Show Hit List

Graded Pokémon cards where a live Alt, Fanatics, Goldin or eBay auction already sits above what the card has actually sold for. A card is marked Solid only when it passes every check below. Everything else says exactly what's missing.

Card Ladder numbersEvery sale comes from Card Ladder (eBay, Fanatics, Alt, Goldin and more), matched on the slab's own label and grade. Best Offers show the price actually accepted, not the ask. Card Ladder price is the average of the card's last 3 sales; CL Value is Card Ladder's own estimate, which can sit far from recent sales when it has little to go on (look at its confidence).
Fees on both sidesAlt and Fanatics add a 20% buyer's fee and Goldin 22%, and their sale prices include it. So each bid is shown all-in before it's compared. eBay has no buyer's fee.
Worst-case buy priceAssumes the dealer at the show asks the highest of the last 3 confirmed sales. Resale assumes the next dealer pays 85% of where this auction already sits.
Solid needs all ofAt least 3 real sales in 90 days, from 2+ sites (or 5+ sales), the latest within 45 days, a gap between +20% and +100%, and at least 10% profit at the worst-case price.
At the table, before you pay
  1. Scan the slab's cert on the PSA/CGC app. Grade, set, number and variant must match this list exactly: 1st Edition, reverse holo and Gold Star all change the card.
  2. Pay no more than Pay at most. That leaves a cushion even if the auction doesn't climb any higher.
  3. Sell after the auction closes. The finished sale is your proof, and the bid can only go up until then.

Sources: live bids from Alt, Fanatics, Goldin and eBay; sales from Card Ladder in US dollars (your Card Ladder app shows CAD, about 1.4× these numbers). Each sale is matched on the PSA/CGC label: same set, number, grade, 1st Edition, reverse holo and Gold Star. The two CGC Pristine cards had no Card Ladder match, so they're unverified.

A sale is held (struck through, not counted) when it's under 45% of the card's recent median, or a Buy It Now over 1.8× it. The 85% resale figure is an assumption about what a dealer pays against a fresh auction comp; change it in your head if your buyers pay less.
